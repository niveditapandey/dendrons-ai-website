AI FinOps

Rein

Discover and govern your AI spend.

Rein sits inside your environment and logs every AI call that runs through it - which model, which team, what it cost, and whether it stayed within policy. Budget controls and spend attribution are built in. AI Governance

Compass

Govern and evidence your AI.

Compass takes the inventory Rein builds and governs it against the frameworks that apply to your organisation - ISO/IEC 42001, MAS FEAT, and RBI model governance guidelines. It covers risk classification, control mapping, Statement of Applicability, and ongoing monitoring. The output is documentation a regulator or auditor expects to see.
